TagMyAudio is a free and open-source application for organizing your music collection. Edit audio tags, choose album artwork, rename tracks and convert audio, one album at a time or across a selection.

Work in album or track mode, with individual conversion settings for the selected items. CUE albums can also be split into individual audio tracks.

## Main features

- edit essential and detailed tags for one track, an album or multiple selected albums;
- search for artwork online, choose a local image or drag and drop one;
- rename tracks using presets and advanced text operations;
- group albums while preserving the intended order of their tracks;
- listen to tracks while working on their tags;
- convert audio to MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, ALAC, Ogg Vorbis or Opus, with format-specific quality settings;
- switch between light and dark themes and adjust interface zoom from 70% to 150%;
- interface and practical guides in twelve languages, with English as the default.

## Supported systems

- macOS 12 or later, on Apple Silicon (arm64) or Intel (x86_64);
- Windows 10 and Windows 11, on x86-64 processors.

The version 1.8.6 distributions were tested by the maintainer on Windows 10, Windows 11, Mac Intel and Apple Silicon.
